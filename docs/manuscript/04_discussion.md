# What the surviving record can support

The surviving journal combines records of technical practice with advocacy about how that practice should be organized. Its authors can document a design choice, a requirement or an institutional preference with considerable specificity. A design diagram can specify where a control runs while leaving its effect unmeasured. A practitioner narrative can show how an evaluation was conceived without providing a comparison group, baseline denominator or replication. Keeping the evidence design in the article register makes these distinctions available for synthesis.

The strongest source comparisons hold several factors fixed at once. The same PDF hash fixes the bytes and page ordering. A matched offprint can preserve the body while changing folios or an editorial wrapper. Matched text and aligned rendered pages offer separate controls on visible content. The Spicer and Arsenault examples show how publisher-defined web placement resolves relationships that the native document alone cannot. They also show why a recovered online article does not fill an absent print folio.

Several historical boundaries remain. An apparent gap can be an unissued period, a known issue whose copy is lost, a surviving excerpt, or a record attributed to the wrong period. Early scanning geometry can conceal text the printed copy retained. Text-readable pages may omit a diagram, and visually measured pages may contain no recoverable writing. A denominator for published issues or original articles requires contemporaneous numbered witnesses, complete independent holdings or another source that actually bears on the missing material.

## Limitations and future verification

The complete text checks are AI-assisted and source anchored. Independent human dual review, inter-rater agreement, OCR error rates and validated automatic article segmentation are unresolved. The linguistic strata reflect source and editorial history together. The nominal issue-level intervals capture sensitivity within the declared comparison, with no probability model for archival absence or cross-period causal change. The page-shape clusters are geometric descriptions. Their stability under refitting does not validate titles such as advertisement, architecture or evidence. No journal-wide causal effectiveness estimate follows from the reviewed case reports.
