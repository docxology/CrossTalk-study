# Abstract {.unnumbered}

*CrossTalk* offers a longitudinal view of how defense software engineering practitioners present methods, evidence and institutional priorities. We built a source-linked archive of {{NOW_PDFS}} retained issue PDFs and {{NOW_PAGES}} physical pages. Native text and admitted optical extraction make {{NOW_SEARCHABLE}} positions searchable, and page appearance is measured throughout the retained frame. Exact article counts are available for {{NOW_EXACT}} retained issues under their attested scopes, including {{NOW_PRIORITY_EXACT}} of {{NOW_PRIORITY_TOTAL}} initially unresolved PDFs. The register contains {{AUDIT_UNIQUE_WORKS}} distinct complete AI-assisted appraisals. Contrasts in vocabulary and page geometry show how strongly some apparent historical changes depend on the extraction layer, issue weighting and editorial layout. Print and publisher-web comparisons also expose substantive work surviving outside the issue PDFs. Across {{NOW_OPEN}} files with open original scopes we examined all {{OPEN_RETAINED_PAGES}} retained pages and counted {{OPEN_RETAINED_COMPLETE}} confirmed complete bodies. The results describe the acquired sources and leave the historical issue and article denominators open.
