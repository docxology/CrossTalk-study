# How the archive was built and checked {#sec:methods}

## Frame, design and chronology {#sec:design}

This is an exploratory literature and multimodal atlas of an acquired publication
frame. The retained NPS series, independent publisher listings, actual PDF bytes,
article observations and reviewed interpretations are separate layers. A record
in a repository does not establish a historically complete issue run. A listed
article does not establish a reviewed contribution. Every result identifies its
eligible frame and its unit.

The expanded source selections and analysis settings were developed after corpus
inspection. Their recorded seeds and input digests make them reproducible, not
preregistered. PRISMA-P and PRISMA 2020 inform transparent selection accounting;
they do not supply a quality score or certify that a systematic review is complete
[@moher2015prismap; @page2021prisma]. The protocol and dated amendments preserve
inspected development material and the conditions for a future untouched
validation sample.

Repository issued dates, dates parsed from titles, printed publication dates and
archive capture dates retain their own fields. Combined issues, supplements,
reprints, alternate scans and unresolved identities are not filled into an assumed
monthly calendar. Historical reconciliation uses explicit publisher assertions
and contemporary schedule evidence. Exact PDF hashes identify duplicate bytes;
similar content only proposes an identity match for review.

## Acquisition and source custody {#sec:selection}

Acquisition is bounded and records the observed locator, source family, request
policy, response, byte count, digest and outcome. Existing verified payloads are
rehashed and reused. New requests use the applicable host policy and spacing;
redirects require a recorded, reviewed relation. Successful peers remain retained
when another source fails. Prepared, completed and unresolved attempts remain
distinct so an interrupted operation does not become permission to adopt unknown
bytes. Each source family's recovery method requires its own qualification and
replay evidence.

The NPS catalog and downloadable manifest remain separate. Modern publisher
cards and archival candidates have independent ledgers; they are not synthesized
into NPS records. A PDF enters an expanded analytical frame only after payload
integrity, source identity and extraction coverage support that admission. An
unavailable request, contact-only card and independently listed gap are different
outcomes. The historical denominator remains unknown unless independent holdings
evidence establishes it.

Every published table or figure binds its consumed inputs, method, settings,
runtime and artifacts. Readers recheck those inputs before accepting a result.
Immutable outputs preserve earlier generations and explicit failures. Source
indexes retain unresolved and unavailable records, while page search admits only
the independently current physical-page scope.

## Text units and recovery {#sec:units}

A physical page is a zero-based position within an exact PDF digest. Its PDF page
label and observed printed folio are separate observations. Native text preserves
source blocks and coordinates. Accepted OCR is a separately indexed derivative;
it is not fused into native counts. Empty recognition, normalized zero-token text,
missing recognition and recognition failure retain different meanings.

The newer language pass uses the recorded Unicode normalization and tokenizer.
It preserves complete per-issue counts, token opportunities, zero-token units and
source-layer identities. The earlier alphabetic-token baseline remains available
under its own definition. Analyses never silently pool prose, references, code,
tables, advertisements and captions into a validated prose category.

Witnessed repository wrappers support a reversible exclusion view. A separate
native-rich view uses the recorded extraction-coverage rule and exact issue
membership. These views change opportunities and sometimes the population under
study. They cannot repair missing historical material or establish that early
scanned issues are represented by later native-rich issues.

OCR accuracy requires independent transcription, alignment rules and separate
character, word, reading-order and downstream-assignment assessments. Those
accuracy estimates are not available here. A source-image comparison raised a
possible rotation-dependent omission; its cause must be tested on actual
controlled raster PDFs before changing the frozen recognition method. More
recognized characters alone do not demonstrate correctness.

## Article discovery and review {#sec:annotation}

Discovery retains exact contents rows and a separate native-font view. The latter
uses large folios and adjacent larger-than-body title text, calibrated on an
inspected 2022 contents page. It is a retrieval heuristic with unmeasured
historical precision and recall. All observed contents pages are excluded from
the subsequent body-title search. A printed folio is never treated as a PDF
index. Repeated headings, cross-layer ambiguity and missing starts remain
unresolved. Departments and notices remain available for genre review.

The ledger distinguishes title observations, unique physical starts, layer
starts, explicit article regions and source-checked interpretations. An interval
up to another title is a candidate reading window, not certified ownership of
intervening pages. Articles can share pages, continue nonconsecutively, or contain
unnumbered insertions. Region order and overlap checks establish internal
consistency; they do not measure boundary accuracy.

Each executed reading records a question, contribution, setting, evidence basis,
claims, limitations, implications and exact supporting or contrary passages.
Source locations retain their layer, PDF digest, physical page and occurrence.
Implementation accounts, tutorials, conceptual arguments and empirical evaluations
are appraised on their own evidence. Reported improvements keep their units and
study designs; incompatible before/after descriptions are not pooled into a
causal effect.

The thematic 2022 comparison and the structured native-rich/early-OCR selections
are development readings. Their specifications and selection failures remain
visible. AI-assisted source checking is distinct from independent human review.
Agreement coefficients, boundary accuracy and semantic-label accuracy remain
unmeasured; methods for those tasks must match their actual annotation units
[@artstein_poesio2008].

## Language and statistical summaries {#sec:analysis}

Frequency rates use complete eligible token opportunities. Issue prevalence and
dispersion reveal whether a frequent term reaches many issues or is concentrated
in a few. Adjacent pairs restart at every page or explicit region; endpoint
opportunities accompany association scores. Diversity uses nonoverlapping
{{LANG_WINDOW_TOKENS}}-token windows, restarted at each unit, with short units and discarded tails
reported. Concordances retain complete target counts and bounded source-linked
contexts.

The expanded exploratory contrast uses two issued-metadata cohorts: years at or
before {{LANG_EARLY_MAX}} and at or after {{LANG_LATE_MIN}}. Intervening and unparsed years are excluded.
The full two-by-two likelihood ratio ranks retrieval candidates; it supplies no
token-independence p value. Smoothed log-base-two rate ratios show direction and
size. Whole-issue resampling preserves within-issue dependence, while leaving
between-issue temporal dependence, source coverage, extraction error and
data-selected vocabulary outside its nominal marginal intervals
[@efron1979; @cameron_miller2015]. The results report these sensitivity views against their corresponding source frames. Complete descriptive counts do not acquire
sampling uncertainty because they are plotted.

Keyword screens and initialism expansions are retrieval instruments. They do not
establish validated topics, meanings, author intent or institutional adoption.
Contexts and contrary examples precede interpretation [@grimmer_stewart2013].

## Visual measurements {#sec:text}

Embedded raster assets, saved placements, native resource observations and
rendered physical pages are different units. Base rasters are measured without
silently compositing unknown masks. Whole-page appearance uses the recorded
rendering scale and white RGB background, then measures pixel descriptors and
spatial summaries on the full render. Policy refusals and missing outputs remain
outside the eligible denominator. A thumbnail is a viewing aid, not a new pixel
measurement source.

Native layout is a separate derivative: span/font/size observations,
character-weighted typography, column-start candidates and the exact union of
native rectangles clipped to the declared unrotated frame. No-native-rectangle
coverage is an observed zero for that measure; missing font size is unavailable.
Neither condition establishes that the printed page is blank. Reported font
metadata and candidate reading order are not independently validated.

Source excerpts support bounded interpretation of visual form and argument.
Raster entropy, nonwhite fractions and font sizes cannot identify a photograph,
an empirical chart, an architecture, an advertisement or a persuasive argument.
Semantic figure roles and figure–text relations need their own source-linked
annotation and validation [@pfitzmann2022doclaynet; @martinec_salway2005]. The cover groups the issue-front glyphs by year and outlines open print scopes. The text abstract shows issue-normalized mixtures and page-weighted era totals. The page-shape abstract pools each aggregate darkness map into a 12-by-17 grayscale grid and draws the cells as vectors.

To test whether open historical dates appeared on misfiled pages, we screened the opening and closing {{PRINT_DATE_EDGE_LINES}} admitted text lines per retained page. The rule retrieves date-bearing lines at or near the journal masthead. We inspected each cross-date candidate on the source-page raster and recorded what the marked date described. The screen measures detection under its text and placement rules. Independent issue copies and actual printed leaves determine custody.

The folio searches use the retained text keyed by source PDF digest and physical page. For November 1999, the publisher's digital insertion and the neighboring printed pages provide known matches. We count nonoverlapping five-token sequences specific to one query page and inspect the corresponding issued page whenever a different issue reaches eight such matches. Clipped re-extraction tests which printed region accounts for the shared sequences. These comparisons assess retrieval on the available text and do not estimate recall for unheld folios or poorly extracted pages.

### Audit the boundaries of surviving bodies

For every physical position in the {{OPEN_RETAINED_PDFS}} retained PDFs with open original print scopes, compare the page image to the reading register, qualified text and established continuations. Count an article body when its opening, ending and any intervening text are present and the issue attribution is supported. Register recognizable fragments, abstract-only regions and disputed attributions separately. Count embedded web copies within the held PDF and identify them separately from surviving print bodies. Preserve links to prior complete appraisals to avoid counting the audit as new reviews.
